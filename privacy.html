Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal-use Google Calendar and Tasks integration of Alfred Personal Assistant, operated by its owner through the GitHub account leojiaoly-rgb.

Google data accessed

With the account owner's consent, Alfred can access calendar-list metadata; calendar event details, such as titles, times, descriptions, locations, and attendees; and task-list and task details, such as titles, notes, due dates, and completion status. Its permissions also allow requested changes to events and tasks. Alfred does not request Gmail or Google Drive access through this integration.

Purpose and use

Google data is used to provide the owner's requested personal scheduling and task-management functions, including identifying relevant calendars, checking conflicts, and confirming requested changes. The integration is not intended to sell Google data, serve advertisements, or build advertising profiles. Google API data is used subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

AI processing and other service providers

Alfred is not a local-only language model. Relevant Google data retrieved for a request may enter the assistant's conversation or tool results and be transmitted to the configured AI provider, currently OpenAI through the Codex connection, to generate a response or carry out the request. Relevant context may also be processed by delegated AI agents when used for the owner's task. Responses delivered through Telegram may contain requested event or task information. Google, OpenAI, Telegram, and any other service deliberately enabled by the owner process information under their applicable terms and account settings. This policy does not claim that those providers have zero retention or that model-training settings have been independently verified.

Storage and retention

Google authorization credentials are stored separately from research notes in private files on the owner's assistant computer, restricted to its operating-system account. This does not imply that every local file is encrypted. Calendar or task details may remain in local conversation history, tool logs, saved notes, or backups where those are enabled. There is currently no integration-specific automatic deletion schedule; retained records remain until the owner deletes or manages them. External providers' retention depends on their own policies and settings.

Control, revocation, and deletion

The owner can revoke Alfred's access in the Google Account third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete earlier conversation records, notes, or provider-held data. The owner can separately remove local credentials and retained records, manage backups, and use applicable provider controls. Deleting a Google event or task is a separate action and should only be performed when requested.

Public website

The public GitHub Pages site contains informational pages only. It has no sign-in form, embedded analytics script, or Google-data viewer. GitHub may collect technical access information as the hosting provider. No Google credentials or personal calendar/task records are intentionally published in the site or its repository.

Contact and changes

Contact the operator through an existing private channel or the non-sensitive contact details, if any, on the GitHub profile linked above. Do not send credentials or private Google information in public GitHub posts. This policy should be updated before materially changing the integration's access or data handling.
