Alfred Personal Assistant

Alfred is a privately operated everyday assistant and research librarian for its owner, Leo. It runs through Hermes Agent and is not offered as a public sign-up service.

What Alfred does

Alfred helps its owner organize personal research, check schedules, and manage personal Google Calendar events and Google Tasks in response to requests.

Why Google access is requested

  • Calendar events: read, create, update, and delete events on calendars the owner is permitted to access.
  • Calendar list: read the list of accessible calendars so Alfred can select the appropriate calendar.
  • Tasks: read and manage task lists and tasks.

This connection does not request Gmail or Google Drive access. Actions are limited to the authorized account's permissions and the owner's instructions. Invitations or messages to other people require explicit permission.

This website contains public information only. It does not display calendar entries, tasks, credentials, conversations, or research notes. The assistant uses external services, including an AI provider; processing is not entirely local. See the privacy policy for details.

Owner and contact

Operated by the owner of the GitHub account leojiaoly-rgb. For non-sensitive questions, use the contact information the owner chooses to make available on that profile. Account-related requests should be made directly to the owner through an existing private channel. Never post Google data or credentials publicly.
